# Gamedev-Journey
Documenting my progress as I try to make the game I would love to play.

## Some stuff about me
Since my childhood I loved learning about, and seeing how PC-s evolve. But since I was never that good at math (nor am I now for the record), the path I choose was of mechanical engineering (because for IT, I would had to take additional math exams, and also my dad is a car mechanic, so family pressure steered me in this direction).

Along the years I had my fair share of designing parts, making technical drawings, and learning a lot of stuff, but I always steered toward coding, so naturally ended up making code for CNC machines and fabricating parts. Eventually I tried getting out of it and trying game development (learning Godot in my free time, making a pong type game with some cool additional mechanics, time flew with it), and when I mustered my courage to do it full time, leaving my old job behind, in 2023 life decided to hit me with a nasty disease (probably something hereditary, nobody knows what causes it really), basically my immune system attacked my colon. So long story short, after 2 months in a hospital and 21kg lost, from 63kg to 42kg (139lbs to 93lbs), I barely left the hospital, living with an ileostomy bag for the rest of my life. Needless to say this messed me up, phisically (as much as I could at least) I recovered, but for the past 3 years I tried recovering mentally from this. Tried finishing my game but gave up every time for different reasons, ultimately, my lack of willingness beeing the culprit.

I want to change all of this though, documenting my journey here as I go.
